How calibrated RAG works

Once all three references are saved in order from smoothest to roughest, live readings are compared with their vibration levels. Green, amber and red are relative to your three sample drives. Recalibrate if you change the phone holder, vehicle, or typical speed. Before all three are recorded, the app shows the uncalibrated experimental score.
